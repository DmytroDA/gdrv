Privacy Policy — GDrv

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What data the app accesses

GDrv requests access to your Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) and your basic profile (email, name, picture) only to show your files and let you manage them from your own computer.

How the data is used

  • All requests go directly from your computer to Google APIs. GDrv has no servers and does not send your data to the developer or to any third party.
  • Files are downloaded only when you ask (open, download, drag out) and are saved on your own computer.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage (macOS Keychain / Windows DPAPI).
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

GDrv's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove an account in GDrv (Settings → Від'єднати), and revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in this site's GitHub repository.

Усі запити йдуть напряму з вашого комп'ютера до Google. Програма не має власних серверів, нічого не передає розробнику чи третім особам, не містить аналітики та реклами. Токени входу зберігаються лише на вашому комп'ютері в зашифрованому вигляді. Відкликати доступ можна будь-коли на myaccount.google.com/permissions.
